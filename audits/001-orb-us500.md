# Audit 001: Opening-Range Breakout on US500 (open-source TradingView rule, author defaults)

**Verdict: FAIL.** The 2021-24 result did not hold in 2025-26, and gross return turned negative out of sample.

## 1. Claim
TradingView open-source "Opening-Range Breakout" by fabledforman: 50.9% win rate, profit factor 1.18, backtest P&L $154k. Author-reported; no period or cost stated. Source: tradingview.com/script/8vjWAdLN-Opening-Range-Breakout/

## 2. Rules (author defaults, unchanged)
- Opening range 09:30-10:15 New York: high H, low L.
- Trade only if range width ≥ 0.35% of price.
- Stop-buy at H, stop-sell at L. Stop at 0.5 × width from entry. Target at 1.1 × stop distance.
- On a stop, reverse once. Flat at 15:45.

## 3. Instrument
S&P 500 index, tested with CFD and micro S&P futures (MES) cost assumptions. The index is a proxy for MES; the rule is applied to the index price.

## 4. Data
HistData SPXUSD tick bid/ask, Oct 2021 to Sep 2026. Feed spreads, not exchange data. Coverage: 60 of 60 months, all present.

## 5. Costs
- MES: 0.70 bp round trip (1x), 1.40 bp (2x).
- CFD: bid/ask at fill minute, 1x and 2x.

## 6. Split
- Development: 2021-10 to 2024-12 (pre-registered). Passed on gross and cost, failed on t (1.60 vs 2.0 needed) and frequency (2.9 trades/week vs 4).
- Holdout: 2025-01 to 2026-09, opened after a logged post-hoc exception. Same rules.

## 7. Results (MES, 1x, per trade)
| Period | Trades/wk | Gross | Net | t | Years positive | Net at 2x |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dev 2021-24 | 2.9 | +3.11 bp | +2.41 | 1.60 | 3 of 4 | +1.71 |
| Holdout 2025-26 | 2.75 | −1.56 bp | −2.26 | −0.99 | 0 of 2 | −2.96 |

Per-year, per trade: 2021 and 2022 positive, 2023 slightly negative (−1.07 bp), 2024 positive (+2.18 bp). 2025 and 2026 both negative (per-day averages −0.9 and −7.1 bp).
Max drawdown: −4.9% in dev, −8.4% in holdout.

## 8. Robustness
Published parameters only. No sweep was run, so this is not parameter selection.

## 9. Replication
- Blind replication (agent given the written spec only) matched the dev result to 3 decimals: gross 3.107, net 1.987.
- External replication (Krueger, MQL5 blog, self-published, unverified): Zarattini/Aziz variant on SPX CFD, 2015-26 gross +0.119 R (t 2.4), net −0.081 R after the author's cost model.

## 10. Verdict
FAIL. The dev edge was real before costs but thin after them, and it disappeared in holdout.
Strongest reason: gross was −1.56 bp per trade in 2025-26, so no cost level can rescue it.

## 11. Disconfirming evidence
- Holdout is only 1.75 years, so the negative result could be a bad stretch. Dev was 3.9 years and positive in 3 of 4 years.
- The rule has a 2023 loss in dev, so this is a regime-sensitive edge, not a dead one everywhere.
- Feed spreads may be wider than exchange spreads, which would make costs look worse than they are. This is the most likely way the verdict is wrong, though it would need gross below −0.7 bp to change the sign.
- A different (non-published) variant might work. That's a new hypothesis, not this audit.
